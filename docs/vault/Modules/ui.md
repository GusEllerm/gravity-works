# Modules/ui

> [!abstract] Role
> The builder UI (brief §9.1): tray, socket snapping, ghost, budget counter. Plain accessible DOM so the stage-6 a11y pass extends rather than rebuilds it. Owner: Systems Engineer.

## What it does

`src/ui/builder.ts` — `createBuilder(host, { level, build, tray?, trayParams?, solids?, onChange })` renders `#gw-tray` (a real `<button>` per kit kind from `PIECE_KINDS`, `data-kind`, aria-pressed; tray kinds' labels are the DECREMPENTING legend "drop ×1" → "×0" — ×N counts what is LEFT to place, the line playtest G read as stuck; since playtest M every button word is the kind's PLAYER word from `PIECE_LABELS` — `gapLip` renders "Lip", `finishCup` "Cup", `springLauncher` "Spring" — with the codename surviving only as the element/`data-kind` id, and locked/spent messages say it in the same word), `#gw-piece-count` ("n of budget pieces used" — **n counts TRAY placements**, never built-in fixtures), `#gw-ghost-state` (EMPTY when nothing is held — the internal `GhostState` words never reach the screen; `GHOST_LABEL` maps them to the VERB TABLE copy), `#gw-target-label`, and the `#gw-place` / `#gw-rotate` / `#gw-remove-piece` / `#gw-reset` / `#gw-launch` buttons (`#gw-reset` is the permanent **Retry** — since playtest N ("Retry only appears in the result panel") the as-built retry lives OUTSIDE the panel: the button word is "Retry", the id stays `gw-reset` (a test surface, not copy), and the shell wires it and the panel's `#gw-result-retry` to the SAME `resetCar` — car home, build kept, panel away, so a dismissed panel never hides the way back). Tray gating (brief §9.3): with a `tray` map, kinds outside it are aria-disabled, a kind's placements are capped at its tray count, `playerCount()` is the budget/result tally, Remove never deletes a fixture and is ONE SPOKEN SHOT PER CLICK since playtest Z round7 (two clicks "eaten" with the counter unmoved read as a mode quietly turning itself off — there is no mode, and every shot now speaks on `#gw-ghost-state`: success `removed the <piece>`, refusal `nothing to remove — the track is empty` / `nothing to remove — only the level’s own pieces are on the track`; the Delete/Backspace keys are the same spoken path — no silent state drift), and the held kind is PLACED with the level's tuned `trayParams` (derived in `src/boot.ts` from each kind's first `parBuild()` placement, or from the level's own `trayParams` declaration for a kind the par line never places — L02's arc-route `gapLip`/`landing`, and since the stage-4 fail-timing pass its locally-tuned `drop`/`straight` geometries reach the tray the same way (one geometry per kind is the contract `trayParityBuild` checks); kit DEFAULTS would build a different gap than the level was par'd on). The target line speaks the same words (playtest M retired "end of `finishCup`"; the R+S sweep retired the internal nouns too — the cup's open exit reads "target: cup on the table", an open release socket "target: the car's start point" (reworded at playtests Y+X round6 — "where the car starts" read as a PIECE name), an exit of a piece whose kind the tray never stocks reads "end of the pre-built X" — a target may name a visible built-in but must never name a greyed kind as if it were placeable (playtest Y item 4, the kitchen02 run-out "end of curve"; swept by `tests/e2e/playtest-y-clickdiff.spec.ts`), never "end of cup"/"level start" — and since playtest Z round7 the line's VERB disambiguates aim from goal: WHILE HOLDING a piece it reads "place drop at: X" (an aim, a spot to put it — "target" was doubling as the star rules' goal), empty-handed the ring keeps the neutral "target: X"), and the R/Flip press answers with a passive "· rotated" tail on `#gw-ghost-state` when the ghost is on screen (playtest M: "R-flip has no visible confirmation text"); with NOTHING held the press is no longer mute either — the FIRST press of a session that ARMS the reversal says what it did, `REVERSING_WHY` — "reversing — track runs backwards this way (press R unless you want a coaster)" (playtest R's K4 wall: one stray ghostless R silently armed the reversal of every later mount, seen only in the diagnosis; the FLIP_WHY discipline applied to the ARM instead of the FIT, once per page session, the next action — the first place included — retiring the line and the held ghost's own copy taking the line over). ONE geometry per kind is the tray's whole contract: a level that chains the same kind at two sizes has a par build no tray can place (`trayParityBuild`, `tests/unit/kitchen-levels.test.ts`). The gating reason is ON-SCREEN, not a hover title: `#gw-tray-reason` states "Greyed pieces are not in this level · ×N counts pieces left to place · pieces already on the track came with the level" (the third clause is playtest Z round7 — "a piece listed with no ×N, yet 4 of 4 used — which pieces did I actually own?": greyed = not stocked, ×N = stock counting down, built-in = the level's own, never in the tray and never counted), every aria-disabled button carries `aria-describedby="gw-tray-reason"`, and clicking a locked or spent button says which in `#gw-ghost-state` (playtest C: "state and appearance disagreed") — and since playtest N the spent/locked lines also name the piece IN HAND when it differs ("no Landing left in the tray — you are holding Lip"), because both M and N read a bare kind-named message as a verdict on the held piece; the companion fix is that hovering a SPENT tray button no longer steals the hold (`mouseenter` selects only a `selectable` kind — the silent hover-swap was the actual origin of the contradiction). Budget copy never doubles the tray: on a TRAY level the per-kind ×0 legend is the counter, and the status line only speaks where the tray cannot say it (sandbox budgets keep "out of budget"). Teachability (playtest A never learnt Place): `#gw-tray-hint` — "Aim: hover the world or ←→ · Place: click the world or Enter · Flip: R · Look: right-drag · Home: press Esc twice" — appears while a piece is held and nothing has been placed yet, and retires at the first successful `place()`; it describes only inputs that actually work (the U round4 tail: "Home: Esc Esc" read as a stuck key, so the line now states the DOUBLE-press recenter honestly — the gesture itself is `build-camera.ts`'s two-Escapes-inside-`RECENTER_MS`, proven by `tests/e2e/camera-torture.spec.ts`). THE RING SPEAKS (playtest Q: "unlabeled white rings"): `#gw-ring-hint` — "the ring is where it will land" — shows the FIRST time the ring is visible in a page session (session-scoped, not save-scoped — a fresh visit re-teaches it) and retires at the first placement; the ring's socket label on `#gw-target-label` shows whenever a target exists, held piece or not, so the ring is never mute. NO STUCK HOLDS (playtest Q: "no Drop left" while holding a spent Drop): placing the LAST of a kind RELEASES the hold and leaves a one-shot line on `#gw-ghost-state` ("last Drop placed — pick another piece") that outlives the async rebuild and retires at the next action, and a spent-kind place refusal releases too — a hold the tray cannot stock never strands on the next Place. FOCUS POLICY (playtests P+Q: "Enter re-picks the last-focused button", "Rotate eats focus", "Enter ambiguously relaunches"): every builder/toolbar button (and the panel's Retry/Next and the Help toggle) `blur`s itself on the `click` event — AFTER the button's native activation — so keyboard focus returns to the world the moment a control fires; Enter's world action (place) fires only with focus on the body/canvas/board group, and Launch keeps its NATIVE Enter only while the Launch button itself is focused (`tests/e2e/playtest-pq.spec.ts`). Budget copy never doubles the tray: on a TRAY level the per-kind ×0 legend is the counter, and the status line only speaks where the tray cannot say it (sandbox budgets keep "out of budget"); ONE counter, ONE verb (playtests P+Q: "0 of 4 used" vs "ready — 1 placed") — the idle line of `runStatusLine` states the SAME tally in the SAME words ("ready — n of m pieces used") and the in-run/terminal lines carry the clock only, never a tally to contradict `#gw-piece-count` mid-session. It owns no physics: every mutation hands the canonical `Build` to `onChange`; the shell rebuilds the `World` (`src/boot.ts`).

## Stage 3 additions (result screen, callouts, help drawer — brief §9.1, §9.3)

`src/ui/result.ts` — the end-of-run panel, shown by `src/boot.ts` ONLY on a terminal status (§5.11: no panels over the set during a run). `createRunRecorder()` samples the `WorldState` stream and keeps five witnesses — apex (highest point + speed there), touchdown pitch, final airtime, last-grounded pitch, last speed-gain time — all derived from state `World` already reports; `physicsNote(result, evidence, actionableKinds, placedKinds)` prints the one-line failure note (§9.1); BUILD-/TRAY-AWARE TAILS (playtest M: "'lower the lip' advice when I had no lip placed"; extended by playtest Q: "'flatten the landing' when Landing isn't in the tray (L2!)"; extended by round-5 playtest W on the PHRASING): `actionableKinds` is the set of piece KINDS the player can act on NOW — placed in the build that ran OR still stocked in the level's tray — computed by `actionableKindsFor(build, tray)` in `src/boot.ts` at the terminal status, and an advice tail may only name a kind inside it, each half gated by its OWN kind (nose-first keeps `flatten the landing` only when a landing is reachable and `lower the lip` only when a gapLip is; the stalled-flat line names the booster only when the booster is actionable; with no reachable kind the honest head stands alone — `tests/unit/result.test.ts` sweeps a K2-style tray and asserts no printable note names an unavailable kind). PHRASING then tracks the three-way truth placed / tray-only / not-actionable (playtest W: a drop-only build that never placed a Landing got "flatten the landing" — logically honest, but phrased as build-critique and read as a lie): `physicsNote` also takes the set of kinds actually IN the build (`placedKindsFor(build)` in `src/boot.ts`), and each half wears CRITIQUE wording ("flatten the landing", "lower the lip") for a kind in the build, ADD wording ("add a flat landing", "add a lip") for tray stock, and is dropped otherwise; an unknown set stays permissive and keeps the shipped wording, so shared/replay pages are unchanged. The sibling sweep left the booster tail as-is — "add a booster" is already add-shaped and honest for a tray-only and a placed booster alike — and no other tail names a kind. Both sets are plumbed UI-side only; the physics and the run hash never see them. `resultModel` pairs the note with `starsFor` AND the scored-against `Par` (see `Modules/world`); `starGlyphs` is the one ★/☆ readout shared with the share card. A finished run gets no note. `outcomeLines(model)` (pure) prints the par transparency the playtesters demanded — "N pieces — par M ✓" and "t s — par T s ✓" with per-line marks on a finished run, the marks quiet otherwise — plus the static star-rules line and `starRulesLine(par)` (exported) is the same three lines with a specific level's par numbers filled in — the teaching line the level select prints per rung and the level's first boot says quietly (playtest N: "the star rules only appear after a run"; teaching precedes failure). REPLAY HONESTY (playtest J: "N pieces — par M" meaningless on a re-run of an already-starred level): the model carries `bestStarsBefore` (the star the save held BEFORE `recordStars` wrote this run's — read in `src/boot.ts` at the terminal status), and a FINISHED run with `bestStarsBefore >= 1` gets the verdict lines instead of the target lines — the run's own numbers lead and the par reads clean: "2 pieces — beat par ✓ (par 3)" / "2.40 s — over par (par 2.21 s)". A FAILURE keeps the failure rules exactly (unmarked tallies + the note, replay or not: on a failed run par is still next attempt's target). `createResultPanel(host)` builds `#gw-result` (`#gw-result-stars` — a `role=img` ★/☆ readout — `#gw-result-time`, `#gw-result-pieces`, `#gw-result-rules`, `#gw-result-note`, `#gw-result-retry`, `#gw-result-next`) inside `#gw-stage`, CENTRED over the stage and scrolled into view on `show` (a stage-CORNER pin landed off-viewport whenever the reader had scrolled to look at the world — the 3/3 "the panel is invisible" finding; the visibility channels are `hidden` AND `display` together, the help drawer's lesson). The panel carries NO inline style (playtest J: at ~960x540 it outran the fold and the Next button was cut off): its whole look is in `src/ui/shell.css`, capped by `max-height: min(calc(100% - 16px), calc(100vh - 16px))` (internal scroll only as a backstop) with a COMPACT pass under `@media (max-height: 700px)` so both buttons sit inside a short window with nothing scrolled — the playtest Z round7 short-window pass: the old 620 px threshold never fired at a real 1280x633 player window (page 962 px tall, panel and caption below the fold, a Next click intercepted `covered by <p#gw-piece-count>`), so the ≤700 px block compacts the panel AND tightens the page chrome AND caps the canvas's width to `calc((100vh - 380px) * 960 / 540)` (aspect-true height = the room the window leaves; a max-HEIGHT cap would squash the buffer and skew aiming), and the panel now rides `z-index: 6`, ABOVE the sticky toolbar, so a scrolled page cannot bury its buttons under the builder lines (asserted at 1280x633 in `tests/e2e/result.spec.ts`). The panel BOX takes NO pointer events (root `pointer-events: none`, `user-select: none`; interactivity scoped to `#gw-result-buttons`) — with the panel open, world clicks land on the canvas around it AND through it, and a drag can no longer select panel text (playtest N: "dragging selected panel text instead"). Retry/Next are the shell's wiring (as-built retry; `nextLevelId` ladder walk in `src/boot.ts` — kitchen01→05, the walk continuing `kitchen05 → bedroom01..04`, `bedroom04 → bathroom01..04`, `bathroom04 → garden01..04` and, since the garage close-out, `garden04 → garage01..04` with `garage04` last and Next-less on the campaign's final rung), and Next is STAR-GATED (`gateNext` in boot): it appears only when the finished run earned ≥ 1 star — a 0-star failure shows Retry only, because §9.2's ladder advances on stars, not on trying (playtests E/F/G: "Next level after a failed run — skipping is allowed"). Both panel buttons BLUR after activation (the P+Q focus policy, `Modules/ui` above): the Enter that retried is never also the Enter that re-fires Retry or re-launches (playtest Q's ambiguous Enter).

The note's coverage map — every line is a function of evidence that exists today; `World` reports `finished`/`fell`/`stalled`/`timeout` and `hazard` joins with the kitchen props (the `hazardsTouched` field and its line exist now so the note cannot lie when it does):

| failure | witness that fires | line |
|---|---|---|
| any + `hazardsTouched > 0` | the count itself | "a hazard took the run" (which one, once props name themselves) |
| `fell` | touchdown pitch < −20° (`pitchOfQuat` at the air→ground transition) | "landed nose first" |
| `fell`/`stalled` | climb > 2 cm AND apex speed < `sqrt(g · climb/2)` — the contact-at-apex floor with the ring radius read off the climb: a computed bound, not a tuned knob | "too slow at the top of the loop" |
| `fell` | final airtime > 0.35 s **AND** takeoff rise `finalTakeoffVy > JUMP_MIN_TAKEOFF_VY` (the deck launched it upward — airtime alone lies: every fall off a counter is airborne ~0.4 s; playtest K) | "flew off after a long jump — the gap outran the landing"; airborne-but-not-launched gets "fell off — the line let go before the cup" (the R+S sweep retired the internal noun "the set" from every player line; the head verb is unchanged) |
| `fell` otherwise | — | "fell off — the line let go before the cup" |
| `stalled` | last-grounded pitch > +10° | "ran out going uphill" |
| `stalled` | a recorded speed gain exists (the last-force-locates witness: the last time speed rose) | "ran out of speed after its last push" |
| `stalled` otherwise | — | "ran out of speed on the flat — friction won" |
| `timeout` | — | "never made it — past the time limit" |
| anything unexplained | — | the catch-all; the note never guesses (§11) |
