# Modules/ui

> [!abstract] Role
> The builder UI (brief §9.1): tray, socket snapping, ghost, budget counter. Plain accessible DOM so the stage-6 a11y pass extends rather than rebuilds it. Owner: Systems Engineer.

## What it does

`src/ui/builder.ts` — `createBuilder(host, { level, build, tray?, trayParams?, solids?, onChange })` renders `#gw-tray` (a real `<button>` per kit kind from `PIECE_KINDS`, `data-kind`, aria-pressed; tray kinds' labels are the DECREMPENTING legend "drop ×1" → "×0" — ×N counts what is LEFT to place, the line playtest G read as stuck; since playtest M every button word is the kind's PLAYER word from `PIECE_LABELS` — `gapLip` renders "Lip", `finishCup` "Cup", `springLauncher` "Spring" — with the codename surviving only as the element/`data-kind` id, and locked/spent messages say it in the same word), `#gw-piece-count` ("n of budget pieces used" — **n counts TRAY placements**, never built-in fixtures), `#gw-ghost-state` (EMPTY when nothing is held — the internal `GhostState` words never reach the screen; `GHOST_LABEL` maps them to the VERB TABLE copy), `#gw-target-label`, and the `#gw-place` / `#gw-rotate` / `#gw-remove-piece` / `#gw-reset` / `#gw-launch` buttons (`#gw-reset` is the permanent **Retry** — since playtest N ("Retry only appears in the result panel") the as-built retry lives OUTSIDE the panel: the button word is "Retry", the id stays `gw-reset` (a test surface, not copy), and the shell wires it and the panel's `#gw-result-retry` to the SAME `resetCar` — car home, build kept, panel away, so a dismissed panel never hides the way back). Tray gating (brief §9.3): with a `tray` map, kinds outside it are aria-disabled, a kind's placements are capped at its tray count, `playerCount()` is the budget/result tally, Remove never deletes a fixture, and the held kind is PLACED with the level's tuned `trayParams` (derived in `src/boot.ts` from each kind's first `parBuild()` placement, or from the level's own `trayParams` declaration for a kind the par line never places — L02's arc-route `gapLip`/`landing`, and since the stage-4 fail-timing pass its locally-tuned `drop`/`straight` geometries reach the tray the same way (one geometry per kind is the contract `trayParityBuild` checks); kit DEFAULTS would build a different gap than the level was par'd on). The target line speaks the same words ("target: end of cup", playtest M's "end of `finishCup`" retired), and the R/Flip press answers with a passive "· rotated" tail on `#gw-ghost-state` — only when the ghost is on screen, because with nothing held nothing rotated (playtest M: "R-flip has no visible confirmation text"). ONE geometry per kind is the tray's whole contract: a level that chains the same kind at two sizes has a par build no tray can place (`trayParityBuild`, `tests/unit/kitchen-levels.test.ts`). The gating reason is ON-SCREEN, not a hover title: `#gw-tray-reason` states "Greyed pieces are not in this level · ×N counts the pieces left to place", every aria-disabled button carries `aria-describedby="gw-tray-reason"`, and clicking a locked or spent button says which in `#gw-ghost-state` (playtest C: "state and appearance disagreed") — and since playtest N the spent/locked lines also name the piece IN HAND when it differs ("no Landing left in the tray — you are holding Lip"), because both M and N read a bare kind-named message as a verdict on the held piece; the companion fix is that hovering a SPENT tray button no longer steals the hold (`mouseenter` selects only a `selectable` kind — the silent hover-swap was the actual origin of the contradiction). Budget copy never doubles the tray: on a TRAY level the per-kind ×0 legend is the counter, and the status line only speaks where the tray cannot say it (sandbox budgets keep "out of budget"). Teachability (playtest A never learnt Place): `#gw-tray-hint` — "Aim: hover the world or ←→ · Place: click the world or Enter · Flip: R" — appears while a piece is held and nothing has been placed yet, and retires at the first successful `place()`; it describes only inputs that actually work. It owns no physics: every mutation hands the canonical `Build` to `onChange`; the shell rebuilds the `World` (`src/boot.ts`).

Placement model (the shell-truth pass, playtests E/F/G): the track is a socket graph. `targets()` lists open sockets (unoccupied `startSocket` + piece exit sockets nothing is joined to within `JOIN_TOL`), in piece-array order. THE TARGET IS ALWAYS VISIBLE: a ring mesh (`builder-target`, a torus at the socket frame) marks the socket the held piece WILL occupy — shown whenever a target exists, piece held or not, and moved by hover AND by the arrow keys (`targetSocket()` / the shell's `__gwTargetSocket` seam prove the marker moves, not a silent index). Hovering the canvas (`attachCanvas(canvas, camera)`, projection-nearest within `HOVER_PX`) moves the marker to the socket a click would use, so the ghost shows the decision BEFORE the click; clicking the canvas PLACES at the hovered socket (click = place, drag optional); with nothing held a click only moves the ring. KEYBOARD PARITY lives on `window` (focus anywhere): ↑/↓ cycle kinds, ←/→ cycle the SAME visible target, Enter places (a focused `<button>` keeps its native Enter — the handler never `preventDefault`s it, or Launch-on-Enter would cancel itself), R flips, Delete removes. **After a successful place the default target moves to the exit the placed piece just created** (the arrows still walk everywhere), and at BOOT the default target is the head of the start-connected chain — `chainHeadIndex` walks the join chain from the first-built piece (every fixture build starts on its start ramp) and aims at its far open exit, deterministically in build order (playtest N's eight-try L01 wall: the old default was list-head `level start`, a legal `drop@start` dead end; the par rail, the ramp exit, is what the first Place must meet — `tests/e2e/playtest-n.spec.ts` proves the three blind Places then build the par line and finish ★★★). Forward seating is `fitSocket` (the contract's exact seating) and the `snapSocket` gate colours the ghost — green `fits here`, amber `flipped fit` (the reverse mount is a half turn about the target's up: deck lines still match, tangents deliberately do not, so the gate reports it honestly), red `no seat at this socket`. The flip ANIMATES: `ROTATE_MS` (150 ms) slerp/lerp snap-to-orientation on `R`, because playtest G pressed it and saw nothing; the e2e pixel-diffs the canvas to prove the ghost region changes. The VERB TABLE at the top of `builder.ts` fixes one verb per event (place/placed for the tray→track event; "fits here"/"flipped fit"/"blocked" for the socket's verdict) and the failure-note wording in `src/ui/result.ts` (the Feel Engineer's) never collides with it — "snapped" and "seated" are retired from the screen. Internally the amber state is named `reversed` (never `seated` — that word now names ONE event, the cup capture in `world.ts`), the ghost is a translucent mesh of `pieceGeometries(kind)` living in the world's scene (`setScene`), and the physics note head in `physicsNote` always EQUALS the status-line verb of `runStatusLine` (fell off / stalled / timed out).

Set-aware guard (stage-3 wiring): an OPTIONAL `solids` option (an array of `THREE.Box3`s the shell extracts from the mounted set's solid dress via `setPlacementGuard` in boot — counter and FILM layers excluded). When the ghost's piece box intersects a solid (2 mm tolerance), the state reads `blocked` and `place()` refuses — the screen copy is `GHOST_LABEL.blocked` ("blocked — the set is in the way"). AABB-vs-AABB on ghost update only — nothing per frame, no raycasts. Kitchen03's bowl-rim socket is the reference case (`set-wiring.spec.ts`).

## Stage 3 additions (result screen, callouts, help drawer — brief §9.1, §9.3)

`src/ui/result.ts` — the end-of-run panel, shown by `src/boot.ts` ONLY on a terminal status (§5.11: no panels over the set during a run). `createRunRecorder()` samples the `WorldState` stream and keeps five witnesses — apex (highest point + speed there), touchdown pitch, final airtime, last-grounded pitch, last speed-gain time — all derived from state `World` already reports; `physicsNote(result, evidence, buildKinds)` prints the one-line failure note (§9.1); BUILD-AWARE TAILS (playtest M: "'lower the lip' advice when I had no lip placed"): `buildKinds` is the set of piece KINDS in the build that just ran — plumbed in `src/boot.ts` from the current `Build` at the terminal status — and a tail that names a piece the player never placed is suppressed (the nose-first line keeps `flatten the landing`, drops `or lower the lip`, for lip-less builds — `tests/unit/result.test.ts` asserts the note contains no "lip" substring for a straight+drop nose-first fall); the plumbing is UI-side only, the physics and the run hash never see it. `resultModel` pairs the note with `starsFor` AND the scored-against `Par` (see `Modules/world`); `starGlyphs` is the one ★/☆ readout shared with the share card. A finished run gets no note. `outcomeLines(model)` (pure) prints the par transparency the playtesters demanded — "N pieces — par M ✓" and "t s — par T s ✓" with per-line marks on a finished run, the marks quiet otherwise — plus the static star-rules line and `starRulesLine(par)` (exported) is the same three lines with a specific level's par numbers filled in — the teaching line the level select prints per rung and the level's first boot says quietly (playtest N: "the star rules only appear after a run"; teaching precedes failure). REPLAY HONESTY (playtest J: "N pieces — par M" meaningless on a re-run of an already-starred level): the model carries `bestStarsBefore` (the star the save held BEFORE `recordStars` wrote this run's — read in `src/boot.ts` at the terminal status), and a FINISHED run with `bestStarsBefore >= 1` gets the verdict lines instead of the target lines — the run's own numbers lead and the par reads clean: "2 pieces — beat par ✓ (par 3)" / "2.40 s — over par (par 2.21 s)". A FAILURE keeps the failure rules exactly (unmarked tallies + the note, replay or not: on a failed run par is still next attempt's target). `createResultPanel(host)` builds `#gw-result` (`#gw-result-stars` — a `role=img` ★/☆ readout — `#gw-result-time`, `#gw-result-pieces`, `#gw-result-rules`, `#gw-result-note`, `#gw-result-retry`, `#gw-result-next`) inside `#gw-stage`, CENTRED over the stage and scrolled into view on `show` (a stage-CORNER pin landed off-viewport whenever the reader had scrolled to look at the world — the 3/3 "the panel is invisible" finding; the visibility channels are `hidden` AND `display` together, the help drawer's lesson). The panel carries NO inline style (playtest J: at ~960x540 it outran the fold and the Next button was cut off): its whole look is in `src/ui/shell.css`, capped by `max-height: min(calc(100% - 16px), calc(100vh - 16px))` (internal scroll only as a backstop) with a `@media (max-height: 620px)` COMPACT pass so both buttons sit inside a ~960x540 window with nothing scrolled. The panel BOX takes NO pointer events (root `pointer-events: none`, `user-select: none`; interactivity scoped to `#gw-result-buttons`) — with the panel open, world clicks land on the canvas around it AND through it, and a drag can no longer select panel text (playtest N: "dragging selected panel text instead"). Retry/Next are the shell's wiring (as-built retry; `nextLevelId` ladder walk in `src/boot.ts` — kitchen01→05, the walk continuing `kitchen05 → bedroom01..04`, `bedroom04 → bathroom01..04` and since the garden pass `bathroom04 → garden01..04`), and Next is STAR-GATED (`gateNext` in boot): it appears only when the finished run earned ≥ 1 star — a 0-star failure shows Retry only, because §9.2's ladder advances on stars, not on trying (playtests E/F/G: "Next level after a failed run — skipping is allowed").

The note's coverage map — every line is a function of evidence that exists today; `World` reports `finished`/`fell`/`stalled`/`timeout` and `hazard` joins with the kitchen props (the `hazardsTouched` field and its line exist now so the note cannot lie when it does):

| failure | witness that fires | line |
|---|---|---|
| any + `hazardsTouched > 0` | the count itself | "a hazard took the run" (which one, once props name themselves) |
| `fell` | touchdown pitch < −20° (`pitchOfQuat` at the air→ground transition) | "landed nose first" |
| `fell`/`stalled` | climb > 2 cm AND apex speed < `sqrt(g · climb/2)` — the contact-at-apex floor with the ring radius read off the climb: a computed bound, not a tuned knob | "too slow at the top of the loop" |
| `fell` | final airtime > 0.35 s **AND** takeoff rise `finalTakeoffVy > JUMP_MIN_TAKEOFF_VY` (the deck launched it upward — airtime alone lies: every fall off a counter is airborne ~0.4 s; playtest K) | "flew off after a long jump — the gap outran the landing"; airborne-but-not-launched gets "fell off the set — the line let go before the cup" |
| `fell` otherwise | — | "fell off the set" |
| `stalled` | last-grounded pitch > +10° | "ran out going uphill" |
| `stalled` | a recorded speed gain exists (the last-force-locates witness: the last time speed rose) | "ran out of speed after its last push" |
| `stalled` otherwise | — | "ran out of speed on the flat — friction won" |
| `timeout` | — | "never made it — past the time limit" |
| anything unexplained | — | the catch-all; the note never guesses (§11) |

`src/ui/callouts.ts` — the §9.3 first-sight system: `PIECE_CALLOUTS` (one line per kit kind — the physics job the picture cannot show) plus `PROP_CALLOUTS` keyed `prop:<name>` for set prop modules to register into; `firstSight(id, store)` returns the line exactly once per player, recording the id in `SaveSettings.calloutsSeen` (`src/save`). `src/boot.ts` fires it with the kind of the first piece ever placed of that kind. `firstLesson(id, text, store)` is the same once-ever discipline for a caller-supplied NON-piece line (the boot's star-rules one-liner, id `rules:<levelId>`, playtest N) — the seen set is shared, the save shape does not move. The registry pattern has two registrants since the bathroom and garden passes: `src/sets/bathroom/index.ts` writes `prop:wetPatch` into `PROP_CALLOUTS` at module load (the hazard lesson Playtest G never found on L04), and `src/sets/garden/index.ts` writes `prop:shadowBars` (the sun-shadow reading rung 01 teaches) and `prop:sprinkler` (the grip-vs-time line of the sprawl rung 03 ships as an always-wet patch) — `ui` itself stays set-free and imports nothing from `src/sets`.

`src/ui/help.ts` — `createHelpDrawer(host, { unlocked?, reducedMotion? })`: `#gw-help-toggle` opens `#gw-help-list`, one `li#gw-help-entry-<id>` per unlocked manifest entry (title — the `PIECE_LABELS` word for pieces, the bare name for props (playtest M: no raw codenames in the glossary either; the entry id stays the codename) — plus the same one-liner), and ONE shared mini-harness canvas (`#gw-help-canvas`, `preserveDrawingBuffer` so QA can read its pixels) draws every entry's mesh spinning in its own cell via per-cell scissor/viewport — not a renderer per row. The shell hosts it OVER `#gw-stage`: the toggle is a quiet, focusable button pinned TOP-RIGHT of the world and the list is an overlay panel (position:absolute; pointer-events only on the controls so world clicks still land) — never inline content at the page bottom (playtest A+F: "Help = collapsed word-button at page bottom"). The spin rides the fixed 12 Hz stop-motion clock (§5.7): `spinAngle(t) = floor(t·12) · SPIN_STEP`; `reducedMotion` (save setting or the media query) renders one still frame at `STILL_ANGLE` and starts no loop. The renderer is created lazily on first open, and a no-WebGL browser degrades to the text list rather than erroring. The COLLAPSED state ships `hidden` AND `display: none` together — the inline `display: grid` used to override the UA's `[hidden]` rule, so the drawer rendered permanently expanded on the deployed page (deployed-page fix, 2026-10-07; the e2e asserts the collapsed style, not just the attribute).

`src/ui/shell.css` — the interim shell skin (deployed-page fix 2026-10-07, ~25 lines, imported by `src/main.ts`): sans system face, canvas full-width/height-auto, tray as one flex row, visible run-status line. Since the playtest J+K pass it also owns the result panel's whole look (the viewport-safe caps and the short-viewport compact media query above — panel styles must not be inline or the media query cannot win) and the level select's lock states (`.gw-level-lockline`, dimmed aria-disabled rungs) and star-rules lines (`.gw-level-rules`), plus the result panel's pointer-event scoping (box `pointer-events: none`, buttons `auto`) described above. Deliberately not a theming system — the stage-6 UI pass replaces it. The canvas now also carries `max-width: 960px` (its BACKING resolution — the letterbox pass made the element stage-width, and a 980px-wide upscale quietly broke the idle visual baseline) and a warm `#efe0c8` background as the first-paint belt to the braces below.

## Shell truth (playtests E/F/G, 2026-10-06)

`src/boot.ts` grew three player-honesty pieces:

- **First paint** — straight after the renderer mounts, one static warm frame (`SET_TOKENS.kitchen.background` sky through the same WebGLRenderer) renders BEFORE `World.create` awaits the physics wasm: the canvas is never black (playtest F: "black screen for seconds"). The e2e samples the canvas from an `addInitScript` the moment the element exists and asserts non-black (`shell-truth.spec.ts`).
- **Hash legibility** — the run hash left `runStatusLine` ("run — t — hash …" is engineer trivia on a player panel): it lives in `#gw-hash-value` inside a `<details>` summarised in one player line — "Same pieces, same run, every time — this code proves it." (playtest M: "determinism fingerprint — same build, same run, anywhere" was uninterpretable; the engineering truth is unchanged), and when a re-run's hash MATCHES a different build's, the disclosure truthfully says "same run — your extra piece never touched the road" (`#gw-hash-note`; why that sentence is honest: `Modules/replay` — the hash folds the CAR bodies' transforms, statics off the path cannot perturb it).
- **Star-gated Next** — `gateNext(stars)`: `#gw-result-next` rides only runs that earned ≥1 star (see the result-panel paragraph above).
- **Boot build = the fixture multiset exactly** (N-wave fault) — `initialBuild` mounts a piece only while that kind's FIXTURE QUOTA (`fixtures[k]` copies, build order) is unfilled, not by the old kind-membership test (`def in fixtures`): a rung sharing a kind between the tray and the fixtures would mount the tray's copies AT BOOT — the reported kitchen02 "tray empty at boot" fault shape (the membership test cannot tell the fixture's copy from the tray's). `trayParityBuild` and `playerPieceCount` apply the SAME quota so the parity probe anchors what the boot mounted. No shipped rung reproduces the fault (every ladder rung's tray and fixtures are kind-DISJOINT today — verified by probe and browser), so the fix is the class plus the gate: `tests/unit/boot-invariants.test.ts` pins, for every rung, boot ⊆ fixtures, table disjointness, and a boot-FULL tray.

## Stage 4 additions (level select — brief §9.2 rooms)

`src/ui/levelselect.ts` — the `?levels=1` page (`createLevelSelect(root, store?)`, routed by `src/boot.ts` after the share fragment; the game shell links to it via `#gw-levels-link`, "All levels", beside the h1). One `<section data-room>` per campaign room with a real `<h2>` heading (the rooms of `src/world/campaign.ts` `CAMPAIGN` — Kitchen, then Bedroom, then Bathroom, in play order), one `<button id="gw-level-<id>">` per rung carrying the level's REGISTRY name (never a mirrored string) and its earned stars via the shared `starGlyphs` readout (an unplayed rung honestly reads `☆☆☆`), and every OPEN rung carries a `.gw-level-rules` line — `starRulesLine` with that rung's own par numbers — so the star rules are legible BEFORE the first run (playtest N: "the star rules only appear after a run"; the level's own first boot says the same line quietly through `firstLesson`). At-a-glance lock states (playtest K: "taught nothing about what's unlocked or why", and a plain-text ☆☆☆ on a locked rung read as "merely unstarred"): a LOCKED rung carries a 🔒 glyph and NO ☆ padding, states its rule INLINE in the button — "Earn a star on <registry name> to open this" — and repeats it on click; stars ACTUALLY earned stay visible even behind a lock (the `?level=` doctrine lets a star exist on a still-gated rung, and hiding a real trophy is as big a lie as a fake ☆); an unlocked-unplayed rung shows the `☆☆☆` placeholder, an earned one its ★ — the three states differ without a hover or a click. The unlock rule is NOT restated here — it is `levelUnlock` in `src/world/campaign.ts` over the save's `progress` record, read once at render (the page never watches storage; a page swap is a page boot). Locked rungs follow the tray's convention: `aria-disabled="true"`, focusable, `aria-describedby` the page's live region `#gw-levelselect-status`, and CLICKING ONE SAYS WHY ("Locked — earn at least one star on <registry name> to open this level.") and never navigates — a locked rung is a talking button, not a dead affordance. The static status line states the rule up front ("A level opens when the level before it earns at least one star."). The page is the PLAYER surface: unlike the recorded `?level=` debug addressing, every button it shows is gated by `levelUnlock`, and nothing it hides is open by the rule.

`src/world/campaign.ts` (lives in `src/world`, drives this page and the shell's Next): `CAMPAIGN` rooms → `CAMPAIGN_LADDER` (the concatenated flat ladder — the SAME sequence `nextLevelId` walks, so Next and the page cannot disagree, and the `kitchen05 → bedroom01` boundary is one normal step with `bedroom04` last), `nextInCampaign`/`previousInCampaign`/`campaignRoomOf`, and `levelUnlock` — §9.2 stated once: unlocked iff first rung, OR the previous rung earned ≥ 1 star (`progress.stars`), OR the legacy `progress.reached` carry (the v1→v2 migrade's record of where a kitchen-era player stood, `Modules/save`; writes of `reached` STOP at the migrade — placing a piece never opens a door). A rung's star is recorded at the terminal status by `recordStars` in `src/boot.ts` (best-per-level; failures record nothing).
