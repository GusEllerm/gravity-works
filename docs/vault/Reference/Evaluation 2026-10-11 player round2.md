## VERDICT — 6.5 / 10. What a stranger meets is better than the 7 build's ledger says, and one of the fixes that earned that 7 is false in play.

The film finally stars the car. The farewell finally wears no chrome. The magic words are on the board.
Those are real, verified, and they matter. But the flagship level-design fix — kitchen02's blocked rim,
the game's one "choice that cannot be faked" — does not survive the shipped Place button: the first
click order a stranger actually performs seats all four pieces, runs the cup, and finishes UNDER par.
The daily rung, examined for the first time as a stranger rather than a reviewer, is a rung-1 with a
date sticker: the game's own physics law says the seed changes nothing. Score moved −0.5 from the final,
not because the game regressed globally, but because two of the surfaces the 7 leaned on are load-bearing
and cracked.

## The cold sixty seconds (measured, 1280×633)

stranger then meets: display-face chrome, post ON, a 574×323 stage. **574×323** — read that against the

over the world, and on a fail it still drops a full-width band across the bottom chrome (bathroom05's
note sits ON the chip strip's line — the disjointness law holds for the idle chrome; the fail band
overlays what it found).

## kitchen02, the flagship fix, in four orders (this round's headline)

The claim in `src/world/levels/kitchen02.level.ts` and the P4 ledger: "the last ask of EVERY dump order
is refused" — all twelve, by the reach-sum law; the three seated pieces then die. In my browser, at
human click pacing:

1. `straight, straight, lip, drop` — 4th ask REFUSED ("blocked — the cup is in the way"), trio launched,
   dies 1.15 s. As advertised. The lesson works when the ring aims at the rim.
2. `straight, straight, drop, lip` — the 4th ask is NOT refused: with the drop seated, the shipped
   default aim points the last piece at **"end of the pre-built curve"** — the fixture run-out PAST the
   cup — where the guard sees no cup-body overlap and lets it seat. 4 of 4 used. Launched: **FINISHED
   1.02 s — beat par, ★★☆, "1 more piece than par."** A two-star under-par payment for clicking every
   tray button, reproduced at both machine and human pacing.
3. `drop, lip, straight, straight` — 4th ask refused; I launched the trio anyway: **FINISHED 1.02 s,
   ★★★, "3 pieces — beat par ✓."** A three-piece off-list build pays a perfect score; the sweep's
   "no wrong build finishes" (~25 000 geometries) never enumerated the seat the shipped ring offers.
4. The same order as (2) on a re-entered page fell at 0.89 s — the same clicks, a different verdict.

So the "choice that must be MADE" is now a coin: the dump is sometimes mocked at the rim ("the cup is
in the way", trio dies) and sometimes REWARDED under par, depending on which open end the ring chose
first. The unit test walks a chain cursor from the ramp exit; the shipped Place button aims a ring, and
the ring walks to the curve run-out. The e2e stands because it replays seat-exact dumps, not ring
targets — the fix is true in the harness and false in the hand. The trap is the one this program
diagnosed itself at k03 ("building backwards from the cup runs off-table") — and at k02 the ring now
BUILDERS backwards by default, its `goal` tell apparently not covering the guarded run-out seat the
dump actually used.

## The ghost bar's truth

The par ghost teaches the clock honestly — deltas, "same pieces as par", the +0.12 sting is real — and
it races nothing at porch01's 1.02 s (over before readable), and it teaches by SPOILING at the puzzle
rungs: on k02, the translucent sedan rolls the lazy line beside you while you decide which way to
build. A default-on answer car is a teacher that reads the answer aloud. The friend-ghost paste flow,
by contrast, is flawless — pasted my link, "their car is on the grid — launch to race it", their build
mounted as track; and the verdict strip on their link said "verified" honestly.

## The daily, and what IS it

`?daily=1` lands on kitchen01 with the date as `build.seed`. The game's own kitchen01 card: "8-seed
sweep bit-stable in time (0 % spread; the seed is folded into the hash only)." The daily run therefore
cannot differ from yesterday's run: same tray, same line, same 2.37 s dump-completable clock (mine
finished, chip: "race today — best 2.37 s · streak 1 day · your own runs on this device only"). The
chip is honest about privacy; the FEATURE is honest about nothing, because "race today" is a race
against a track that is not today's — it is rung one's, forever. One rung, no rotation, no variance:
the emptiness risk is not a risk, it's the design.

## Sandboxes at 99 stock, 10 real minutes

All thirteen kinds stock ×99 — the whole kit is TOUCHABLE, and that alone is more toy than the campaign
carries. But the affordances mislead a freebuilder: the default ring's first target on an empty sandbox
is **"the car's start point"**, and a stranger's first loop seats ON the spawn ramp; three loop
builds in porch-sandbox died at ~1.3 s with campaign-shaped advice ("add a flat landing or add a lip ·
place at: cup on the table"), and the loop only finally RAN in kitchen-sandbox after I guessed the
booster run-up — then it ran END TO END, 2.09 s, my best time anywhere. The 2.30-R law is real and the
game never says it exists. Does freebuild with no par collapse into dumping-and-praying? Worse than
that: the sandboxes SHIP with a par (stars, "at or under 4 pieces · 1.20 s") pasted onto freebuild, so
the dump IS the scored line and the actual toy is the unscored physics; and `?levels=1` names the six
sandboxes in one true sentence with no link on it — you still need the farewell or a URL.

## Sound in hour two, and the 2am question

What plays is roll-noise under each run, surface-parameterised thuds, one landing tick per contact, a
ring ping per loop, and a three-note star chime per finish (instrument-verified in code, never by my
ears — the harness is mute to me; stated limit). The repetition risk is the chime: on a sandbox grind
the same three notes land every 2–3 s of finished run; at ten finishes that is a metronome of
congratulations. Would I keep playing after the campaign? Honestly: I'd finish the ladder in an evening
because the teacher is superb, send one link that shows a real car, do the daily once to confirm it is
a sticker, and then LIVE in the sandboxes — because loop physics works and nothing else in the game
lets you feel it. The game does not, at any point before the farewell, tell a tinkerer that half exists.

## Top eight brutal truths

1. **The flagship choice rung pays a stranger for not choosing.** Scenario: a player clicks k02's four
   tray buttons top-to-bottom, launches — 4 of 4 seat (the ring aims the last piece past the cup onto
   the curve run-out, where the guard does not stand), the run FINISHES 1.02 s under par, two stars.
   The other order gets "blocked — the cup is in the way" and a 1.15 s death. Same clicks in different
   order, opposite verdicts: the game's honesty about the choice is now order-lottery. Cause: the goal
   guard tests the seat the test harness walks, not the seat the shipped ring aims at.
2. **The off-list trio pays three stars.** Scenario: dump `drop, lip, straight, …` — the last ask is
   refused, the three seated pieces LAUNCH and FINISH 1.02 s "beat par" ★★★. The file's "~25 000
   geometries, no wrong build finishes" never enumerated the ring-aimed seat. Cause: level-data truth
   tested by cursor arithmetic, never by the shipped default-aim path.
3. **The daily is a date sticker on rung one.** Scenario: a returning player opens the daily on Tuesday,
   dumps k01's three pieces, 2.37 s, chip logs a streak; it was the same run on Monday, and will be on
   Sunday, because the seed "is folded into the hash only" by the game's own physics law. Cause: T3.4
   rode the seed rails and the rails go nowhere.
4. **The par ghost reads the answer aloud at the puzzle rungs.** Scenario: on k02, before I have chosen
   a way, the translucent sedan rolls the lazy line beside my builder — the game's default-on teacher
   demonstrates the solution. Cause: default-ON par ghosts and choice rungs were never held in one
   design room.
5. **bedroom02 still asks for a verb the game does not have.** Scenario: dump dies 2.33 s, note says
   "flatten the landing past the cup"; I remove, rotate, replace — every execution of the sentence's
   nouns dies (rotated: 1.30 s). Four days, three evaluations, the same unspeakable fix. Cause: advice
   vocabulary (flatten) still ahead of builder vocabulary (place/rotate/remove).
6. **The encore rail is one rung in four paints, to the centisecond.** Scenario: bathroom05 dies at
   2.63 s; bedroom05 — same tray, same death — 2.63 s. And the finale, porch05, finishes by dumping the
   tray in 1.36 s, so the farewell's crane flies me out of a rung that asked nothing.
7. **Fixing the farewell's leak made the world smaller.** Scenario: the same 1280×633 laptop that got
   613×345 in October now gets 574×323 — the reserved chip band the leak-fix bought is paid in canvas,
   and the crane pass it protects glides between rooms on green discs floating in grey void.
8. **The sandboxes are the toy, and the game hides the door.** Scenario: ten minutes at ×99 stock — my
   first loop lands ON the spawn ramp because the ring says "the car's start point"; three honest loop
   attempts die with campaign advice; the loop finally runs end-to-end only after I guess a booster
   run-up — and the sandbox stamps stars and a par on the freebuild anyway. The level board names the
   six sandboxes and links none.

## Where the program's fixes made something WORSE

Three of the fixes traded a true statement for a luckier one. The blocked-rim guard is the worst trade:
before, the union dump was a stable lie — it always finished and the star row quietly taxed it; now the
same clicks are sometimes mocked at the rim and sometimes rewarded under par, depending on which open
end the ring happens to aim at first, and the program's own note asserts the twelve-order refusal the
shipped button demonstrably does not enforce — a fixed bug wearing a green badge is worse than the bug,
because nobody will open that file again. The chip-band reservation bought honest chrome by spending 20
vertical pixels of world at the most common laptop size — the stage got smaller while the claim stayed
"54 %". And the default-ON par ghost bought hour-two racing at the price of spoiling the one rung that
was trying to be a puzzle — the best new teacher in the game mutters the answer during the exam.

## Score

**6.5 / 10.** The social loop is finally honest (sedan in the film, verified link, real player), the
ending lands, and the failure-teacher remains the best I have met in the genre at three executed
sentences out of four. But a stranger at the second rung can be paid two stars for not choosing, the
"daily" is one rung with a calendar, and the sandbox — the actual toy — is gated behind finishing the
corridor and an uncirculated sentence. Cheapest points, in order: (1) make the goal guard refuse the
curve run-out too (or aim the ring below the mouth plane on guarded rungs) and add a shipped-aim e2e
that clicks the tray like a stranger; (2) put ONE sandbox link on the level board; (3) rotate the daily
across six rooms by the date, or retire the word "daily"; (4) give the failure note's verb "flatten"
a key — one rotate-step or a "lower it" ask.
