# Copy pass 2026-10-08 (Creative Writer, stage 6)

> [!abstract] Scope
> Every string a player can read, swept against the stage-6 quality bar: short, concrete, verb-first,
> never explaining what a picture already shows; the calm dry-studio voice (no exclamation, no "just",
> no hedging, no shouting); no internal nouns (`socket`, `set`, `budget`, codenames) on any surface.
> Surfaces swept: boot status lines (`runStatusLine`) and page chrome, dev-preview badge, hiccup line,
> determinism disclosure, tray buttons + legend + hint + tie tails + ghost verdicts + every refusal line,
> the ring line, §9.3 first-sight callouts (13 pieces + 7 props), the result panel (stars/time/pieces/
> rules + every fail-note branch incl. the add-* advice tails), help drawer, level select (room headings,
> lock rule, star-rules line), share card, the replay/share page (title, tagline, verification section,
> winding, speed, event ticks), sound controls, and every error state.
> ~90 strings reviewed, 14 changed. Nothing changed gameplay behavior; tray-advice (fail-hint law) and
> the whole unit suite stay green; the one spec asserting a changed exact string is updated below and
> justified there.

## Reviewed and kept (representative — the pass found these already at bar)

- Status lines: "ready — N of M pieces used", "fell off — t", etc. (one counter, one verb; the idle tally
  equals the builder's count line); head verbs equal the note heads (verb table).
- Refusal lines already concrete and honest: "nothing in hand — pick a piece from the tray first",
  "nothing fits out here — click nearer the ring or an end of the line", "nothing to remove — the track is
  empty", "removed the drop", "last Drop placed — pick another piece", the locked/spent tray lines that
  name what is IN HAND, `FLIP_WHY`, `REVERSING_WHY`, "the ring is where it will land".
- The tray hint chord and the tie tails — pinned by three specs and exactly the affordances the keys have;
  `]` named as one key everywhere.
- All thirteen piece callouts except #1/#2, and the wet/hazard notes' physics halves ("halves grip",
  "put your line around it, not through it").
- The fail-note machinery: nose-first/loop/jump/uphill/last-push/flat lines, the add-vs-critique phrasing
  law (add-only tails name tray stock; "add a flat landing", "add a lip", "add a booster"), drive-off
  tail, "re-place it flat (no R)", "a hazard took the run — line up to miss it", the catch-all
  ("never a guess" — its hedge IS the honesty).
- Level select: room headings, "A level opens when the level before it earns at least one star",
  "Earn a star on X to open this", the star-rules line (the "(par = the target time for this run)"
  gloss is three-time spec-pinned and does name the clock — kept).
- Panel/share chrome: "Retry", "Next level", "Share this run", "Card PNG", "copy the link below",
  "this browser could not build the link", "card downloaded", "graphics hiccup — click to restore",
  "dev preview — progress from here won’t unlock anything", "Why the same build always runs the same
  way", "same run — your extra piece never touched the road", "Sound: on/off", "invalid share link",
  "✓ verified on this machine"/"⚠ differs on this machine", replay "Watch this run"/"Build your own"/
  the event tick words ("Launch"/"Top speed"/"Big air").
- All level names except #14 — the new encores included: "Cable Snake", "Twin Drains", "Two Shadows"
  are concrete objects of their rooms.
