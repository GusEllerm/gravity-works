## Suffocation log

1. **Every invented goal is over par BY CONSTRUCTION.** The sandbox par (5 pc / ~2.7 s) is calibrated to the default
   course; my bedroom self-authored 10-piece across-the-room goal finished — and scored ★☆☆ `over par` (22:18).
   Free play reads as failing on the score screen. This is the finding.
2. **`nothing past it is ever travelled` is physics here, not a warning.** Seven straights toward the porch door
   (22:32) changed the run ZERO (identical 0.89 s death). The toy spends my creativity and refunds nothing.
3. **The single-line law**: everything must grow from one free end; hovering furniture says `fits here` while the
   click places `at end of straight` — free placement is refused without ever saying so (bedroom 22:17, bathroom 22:22).
4. **The scoreboard colonizes the sandbox.** Six sandboxes, six inherited pars + a par-ghost that races me whether
   or not I asked (ks-06). A "free" board with an always-on scoreboard is a rung by another name.
5. **Friend variants cannot be scored.** Ghost links race a car but the verdict is ALWAYS `you vs par`; variant-racing
   a friend's remix of my board has no winner, no Δ, no side-by-side line (22:43–22:46, ks-05/09). The payload is
   enough to REPLAY, not enough to COMPETE.
6. **No skill channel**: run time is a pure function of build (+ collider ORDER — my kitchen line moved 2.84→2.72 s
   when I rebuilt it piece-order-differently, the known order-sensitivity Deferred item, sighting 22:11). Self-set
   time trials are unwinnable-by-playing; "drive better" does not exist (steering parked, `Reference/Steering spike`).
7. **Advice so good it authors the play.** Garage's ★★★ was the advice's own sentence executed by my hands
   (`add a lip … place at: end of ramp`). Free play converges onto par through the hints — spark #2's shadow.
8. **The dump law (5 sightings, timestamped): 22:13:40 kitchen, 22:19:04 bedroom, 22:25:43 bathroom, 22:27:55
   garden, 22:30:31 garage.** Dumps begin the moment a board has NO named goal — after wiping the cup, or facing an
   oversized deck with a tiny goal. The paralysis signature is not difficulty, it's a permissive tray without a stake;
   the toy's own verdicts kept narrating my noise with clinical care (`the curve sits past the cup — pull it back`).
9. Chrome traps: `?date=YYYY-MM-DD` alone silently degrades to a PLAIN rung (no board, no chip) — I played a whole
   "daily" believing it was one (22:33); Launch clicks behind an open failure band swallowed ~6 runs across rooms;
   after Remove-all the advice still says `before the cup` with no cup on the board.

## Verdict for the director's question (evidence-shaped, not design)

The spark is real but NARROW: it lives in (a) the physics reading my intentions honestly (the loop, the jump),
(b) the advice dialogue (it makes building feel conversational), and (c) the wipe-the-board/cup-place moments where
the toy briefly lets me author the GAME. The suffocation is systemic: the scoreboard (par/stars/ghost) is welded to
the DEFAULT course, so freedom scores as failure; the single-line law plus the cup-stop rule silently void any goal
beyond the cup; and dumps happen in EVERY room within minutes once the named goal is gone — which is the strongest
argument that free-form needs a STAKES-MAKER, not more pieces (the ×99 everything-tray is already shipped and was
never the bottleneck). The daily chip proves the recurring-hook machinery already exists; the campaign's tail rungs
prove budgets make the same parts mean something. Whatever replaces the ladder should spend exactly those two
existing assets — named budgets and the race-today chip — on goals the player places.
