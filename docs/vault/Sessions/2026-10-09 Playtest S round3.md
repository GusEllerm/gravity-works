# Playtest S round3 (fresh-eyes browser)

## 1. How far, in order
- K1 Book Drop: **1 launch, cleared** (2★ — missed 2.25 s par by 0.12 s).
- K2 Two Ways: **1 launch, cleared** (3★) — but only after a camera detour forced a full rebuild.
- K3 The Bowl: **3 launches, all failed** ("fell off the set", identical 2.74 s). Wall. Never saw The Tap or any other room.

## 2. What taught placement / order / unlocks
"fits here" while hovering, the `target: end of X` line, and the ring were the real teachers — I never once needed the result-screen card. Unlock rule clicked only on the All levels page ("Earn a star on The Bowl to open this"); nothing in the build view hints at chaining rooms.

## 3. Looking around
Discovered right-drag by trying it in K1 — yes, it orbits. **No accidental placements**: left-dragging while holding a piece only slid the ghost, counter stayed 0. But the camera later zombied itself into a grid of spare pieces (a parts-bin void) and **no drag/wheel combination recovered it**; only a page reload — which silently wiped my in-progress K2 build.

## 4. Best moment (picture)
K2 three-star: ramp, drop, one straight, lip — car skipping across the whole table into the cup in 1.02 s. Caption: "three pieces, one skip, toast on the other side."

## 5. Stuck / guessed
K3 entirely: guessed Drop placement blind; guessed Landing location ("fits here" appeared everywhere on the floor, yet it always snapped onto the chain *behind* the cup — I never found how to lay a catcher under the flight). Rotate's "flipped fit… not for a launch" taught me rotation semantics only after a wasted placement.

## 6. Uninterpretable words/numbers
`target: end of cup` appeared before anything touched the cup; "ready — 4 of 5 pieces used" disagreed with header "3 of 5" after Remove; par "2.65 s" is meaningless noise to a first-timer.

## 7. Broken interactions
Camera wandering into the parts-bin void unrecoverable; reload destroys builds; Landing ghost says "fits here" at every floor point.

## 8. Camera during runs
Pulls way back to a top-down spotlight; car is a speck. The result text overlays exactly where the car died — I never saw where K3's line let go.

## 9. Friend moment
Friend watches my fifth identical K3 fail screen. Caption: "It keeps politely saying 'the line let go before the cup' — it just won't show me where the cup is."
