## The economy (no port was needed)

The set was already registered by the Environment Artist's production
pass, so this ladder is pure level work: the geometry economy is the
bathroom's VERBATIM (ramps 0.22/0.28/0.30/0.26, `KITCHEN_GAP` as

which means the garden clocks ARE the bathroom clocks. Stated openly: the
rung LESSONS, the hazards and the sun regime are the new thing, not the
holes — the same honesty the bathroom pass ran on, and the tests pin the
relationships (choice wins on both mountings, wet beats its own dry and
still loses to height, 24/24 order sweeps), not folklore seconds.

`gardenSetPlacement` (`src/world/setPlacement.ts`) is the bedroom rule with
the WIDEST offset of the four sets, `GARDEN_AXIS_OFFSET` 0.52 m: variant B
dresses all AROUND its deck and the hose coil stands in a forward sun
stripe (its torus reaches set z +0.39), so a bathroom-sized offset lands
the coil 5.5 cm in front of the corridor centre. The flush paving (`DECK_Y`
= 6 mm, the floor-camera law) rides 5 mm under the LOWEST authored
deck-surface; yaw 0 on every rung (the ratified bore is deliberately
off-axis — a level yaw that aimed it at the lane would undo the AD's focal
fix and swing the can onto the corridor; the shadow bars must keep running
ACROSS the lane as reviewed). The dress-guard check is DERIVED, not
analytic: every `dress` mesh's live group box must clear the lane by
10 cm at every mount (at 0.52 the hose's near edge lands at −0.13).
