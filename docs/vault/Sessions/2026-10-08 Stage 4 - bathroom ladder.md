# Stage 4 — bathroom ladder (Level Designer) — 2026-10-08

Four rungs on the ratified bathroom set, entering the campaign after
`bedroom04`, plus the SET PORT that made them possible. Same invariants as
the bedroom pass ([[Sessions/2026-10-07 Stage 4 - bedroom ladder]]): tray =
the multiset `parBuild` uses, ONE geometry per kind per level, ≤ 4 tray-basis
par pieces, every par finishing headless through `src/replay` under the
shipped launch defaults, pars regenerated by `scripts/gen-pars.mjs`.

## The port (verdict: a minimal art port, honestly)

Variant A — the porcelain cathedral, AD 14/15/15 at both canonical cameras
(`Reference/Review 2026-10-08 Stage 4 bathroom+bedroom.md`) — moved from the
throwaway `src/dev/scenes/bathroom.ts` into `src/sets/bathroom/data.ts` +
`src/sets/bathroom/index.ts` on the kitchen/bedroom pattern. Geometry and
materials rode over verbatim; the token-adjusted fill pair and shadow tint
are entered at token level (`BATHROOM_TOKENS`), every prop kept its ratified
pose EXCEPT two dressing positions documented in place (the towel stack to
`TOWELS.position` (−0.32, −0.1) and the soap dish to z −0.24) because the
dev scene dressed a room AROUND a track line running through the set origin
and production mounts the set UNDER a straight +x lane — the straddling
poses land inside the corridor guard boxes. `SOCKETS`/`HAZARDS` ship EMPTY
(the drain is a mouth, not a bore; the wet films are decoration — live zones
are level data, the kitchen04 convention). The registry row went into
`src/sets/index.ts` additively; `bathroomSetPlacement` (`src/world/setPlacement.ts`)
is the bedroom rule with `BATH_AXIS_OFFSET` −0.25.

Friction the port named instead of hiding (art/TA lane, none of it tuned
away in the port):

- **The ceramic three-band RAMP lives in the class, not the scene.** The
  ratified stills were rendered with a scene-side ramp override
  (thresholds [0.28, 0.72], softness 0.06); `src/render/materials.ts`
  carries [0.25, 0.62], softness 0.08. The production set renders with the
  CLASS ramp — porcelain bodies read a touch deeper in the mid-band than the
  ratified PNGs. Per stage-3 decision 5 the port did NOT re-add the
  scene-side hack; if the AD wants the dev numbers, they move into the
  class (one row, art lane), not back into the set.
- **The key light moved owners.** The dev scene owned a cool 45° key
  (`#DDE9F6` @ 1.22, 4096 map); production rigs keys through
  `src/render/lighting.ts`, which takes exactly those dials — `KEYLIGHT` in
  `data.ts` is the recommended rig-options row so a staging scene can pass
  them. A staging scene that skips `KEYLIGHT` gets the warm stock key and
  the wash comes back.
- **Shadow extent is scene-side.** The dev frustum was ±0.7 m; the render
  rig's ortho extents are kitchen-sized and NOT silently grown — a later-
  set rig task, same as the garden's.
- **Yaw is off the table for alignment tricks.** Any yaw that maps a wet
  film onto a lane-centred zone swings the TUB shell across the corridor
  (the prop cluster spans ±0.29 m of the set origin AROUND the dev track).
  This is why all four mount rows are yaw 0 and why film↔zone alignment is
  a data ask, not a rotation.
