## (3) The note tail for the dominant family (three-way rule)

`physicsNote`'s plain drive-off branch now ends "; add …" listing the
kinds with TRAY STOCK LEFT — `stockedKindsFor(build, tray)` in boot, a
fifth argument, plumbed UI-side like the other two sets. It is the
three-way rule at its strictest: a stocked kind is one press away, so
the tail is ALWAYS ADD-shaped; a used-up or absent kind can NEVER be
named; unknown stock (share pages) keeps the bare head. Per build:
deck-only → "…add a drop or a landing" (the lesson), almost-right →
"…add a straight", sink-at-start → "…add a straight or a drop", spent
tray → head alone. AA's six builds print FIVE different lines across the
families; one informed retry each. Note-gate sweeps extended in
`tests/unit/result.test.ts` (K2-style tray never names a landing; stock
is a subset of actionable; a placed kind with spares stays listed).
