# 2026-10-06 Playtest I (confirmation)

Fresh-eyes playtest of the deployed game (kitchen levels), no prior instructions. Evidence = screenshots in the playtest session; a stranger's read, confirming the onboarding loop.

1. **Levels finished, in order:** `kitchen01` (3 launches — fail, fail, 2 stars), `kitchen02` (1 try, 2 stars), `kitchen03` (1 try, 3 stars), `kitchen04` (1 try, 2 stars), `kitchen05` (1 try, 2 stars).
2. **How placement was learned:** the Help picture-dictionary (one sentence per piece), the tray hint "Greyed pieces are not in this level · ×N counts the pieces left to place", and the live line "fits here · target: end of `gapLip`" while holding a piece. The real teacher was failure copy: "Fell off after a long jump — the gap outran the landing."
3. **Best moment (picture):** `kitchen03` three-star finish — the red car dives off the book-ramp, dips through the blue cup at the plate rim, sails past the fork, slaps onto the green landing beside the chopsticks and rolls into the green-rimmed cup as "Finish!" and three stars fade onto the dark panel, spaghetti plate glowing behind.
4. **Where I guessed:** all of `kitchen01`. "level start" as a socket sounded right and wasn't; ←→ cycling landed on "target: end of `finishCup`" and teleported a `drop` to the far cup, pieces scattered near the chopsticks; the ghost then claimed "fits here" everywhere.
5. **Couldn't interpret:** "blocked — the set is in the way"; the status element reading "RetryNext level" while nothing on screen said that; "determinism fingerprint — same build, same run, anywhere"; the ✗ next to "2.77 s — par 2.40 s" only made sense after reading the stars line twice.
6. **Broken interactions:** world-click to place does nothing even when the ghost says "fits here" (Enter/Place worked); rapid Place→Hold chains misattributed ("no drop left in the tray" while holding `gapLip`); the results panel leaves a stale "target: end of `finishCup`" line on screen.
7. **Camera during runs:** it snaps hard-zoomed onto the car and chase-pans through woodgrain and soup-fog so you can rarely tell where you are, then leaps back to a wide shot the instant the run ends.
8. **Send to a friend?** Yes — at `kitchen03`'s three-star ding, screenshot in hand: "Steinway's Kitchen: a toy car, a spoon, and a cup of coffee — 2.59 seconds of pure audacity."
